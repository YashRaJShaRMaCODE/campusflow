# CampusFlow — one-page product brief

## What it is today

A Django API sample for school-scoped exam marks, class summaries, filtered private CSV exports, and minimal export/change audit metadata. The public product page uses synthetic data. It is not a complete student-information system and is not ready for real student records.

## Buyer and pain hypothesis

Small schools may need a focused marks/reporting service that fits around their current workflow. This is unvalidated. A school may instead require a complete SIS, procurement approvals, guardian communication, attendance, and long-term support.

## Possible offer

After privacy/operations gates, test a bounded paid pilot or annual school license for a defined marks/reporting workflow. Do not set price or promise compliance/savings before school interviews, qualified review, and a written data-processing agreement.

## Why consider it

School-scoped permissions, aggregate-only class summary, CSV export history, and an explicit synthetic-data demo. No customer or paying pilot is verified.

## Next proof

Interview administrators and teachers; identify the payer and procurement path; validate data handling with qualified counsel; configure PostgreSQL, monitored backups and tested restore; then test a synthetic end-to-end staff journey before any approved pilot.
